A calm work dashboard, dark first, in black, grey and white: filled cards with clear edges, round buttons and labels, and one red, #ff0000, kept for where you are, what is most urgent and what is unread.

Every name and number on this page is a sample. The fragments are the app's own components, drawn with this direction's colours, fonts, corners and shadows.

Busy, which is not off

Off: a half-strength button, a flat grey field and a pointer that says no. Busy: the same half strength, the wait pointer, and words that say what it is doing. The build makes the strength one value. In the app most disabled fields are busy ones, so the grey box waits until a busy control says it is busy, which is a ruling for the PM and not a call for you.
